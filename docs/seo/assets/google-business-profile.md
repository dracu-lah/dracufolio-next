# Google Business Profile, the one-sitting version

The full reference is `../03-google-business-profile-and-reviews.md`, including the 20 service
areas, the photo list and the week 1 to week 8 review plan. This file is the order to do it in
and the text to paste. Roughly 90 minutes.

## Read this before you start

Google's rule is that an eligible business **makes in-person contact with customers during its
stated hours**. A business that only ever works online is not eligible, and that is the ground
a suspension is argued on, not the address.

So this is honest only if you actually meet clients: at their shop or office in Thrissur, at a
cafe for a first meeting, at their place to hand over and train them. If you do, you qualify
as a service-area business and everything below applies.

If every client you have ever had was purely remote, do not create the profile yet. Do the
first in-person client meeting, then create it. A suspended profile is much harder to get back
than a late one is to start.

Never fake the address, the name or a review. Those are the three things Google reverses, and
the reversal costs more than the listing was worth.

## 1. Create it

`https://business.google.com/create`

Business name, exactly this and nothing more:

```
Nevil Krishna K, Full Stack Developer
```

No "Best", no "Thrissur", no "Web Design Company" appended. Keyword stuffing the name is the
most reported violation and any competitor can file it.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Primary category | `Website Designer` |
| Additional | `Software Company` |
| Additional | `Web Developer` |

Primary category decides which searches you can appear in at all. It can be changed later
without penalty, so test it at three months.

## 2. Address

"Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" Answer **No**. That makes it a
service-area business and no street address is shown publicly.

Google will still ask for a mailing address to verify. Enter your real home address in
Thrissur. It is never displayed. Tick **Hide address** if offered.

Then enter the 20 service areas from `03`, section 3. Thrissur first.

## 4. Hours

Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 19:00. Sunday closed.

Do not set 24 hours. An always-open service business gets flagged.

## 5. Verification

Take **video verification** if offered, it usually clears in a few days.

One continuous recording, no cuts, under 2 minutes, showing three things in this order:

1. **Where you are.** Start outside, show the street or the building.
2. **The tools of the trade.** Walk to the desk, show the laptop with `https://nevil.dev`
   open, show a phone running one of the apps, show a notebook or invoice with the name on it.
3. **That it is you running it.** Turn the camera to your face, say your name and what you do.

If it is rejected, the appeal link in the rejection email works. Re-record with more of the
surrounding area.

Nothing is public until this passes. Fill in everything below while you wait.

## 11. Reviews

Only after verification passes. The review link does not exist before then.

Ask for it: **Ask for reviews** in the profile. Copy the `https://g.page/r/.../review` link and
save it. Test it in a private window, it should open the star picker directly.

Then ask real people who genuinely worked with you, two or three a week, never a batch. The
list of who to ask and what to ask them to mention is in `03`, Part B. Target 5 in month one.

A burst of reviews on a brand new profile is exactly the pattern the spam filter looks for, so
the slow pace is the point, not politeness.
