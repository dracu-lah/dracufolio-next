# 10. Outreach templates

Ready to send. Replace anything in `[square brackets]` and delete nothing else.

Two rules for all of them. Keep the message shorter than the reply you want. Ask for one thing.

A note on the brackets: a message with an unfilled `[name]` in it is worse than no message. Read
it once before sending.

## 1. Cold WhatsApp to a local business, English

**Channel**: WhatsApp, to a number from their Google listing or their shopfront.
**Opener**: their business name in the first line, so it does not look like a blast.
**When to use**: after you have actually looked at their site or noticed they have none. Never
as a copy-paste run down a list of numbers.

```
Hi, is this [business name]?

I am Nevil, a developer based in Thrissur. I looked up [business name] and [noticed there is no
website when people search for you / your site takes about 9 seconds to load on a phone].

I build websites and apps for businesses here. If it is useful, I can send you a free check of
what is slowing you down, with three fixes. No cost and no obligation.

Should I send it?

nevil.dev
```

## 3. Cold email to a Thrissur or Kochi agency

**Channel**: email, to the founder or the tech lead, not to `info@`.
**Subject**: `Freelance frontend capacity, Thrissur`
**When to use**: agencies have overflow and no bench. This is the highest-conversion cold
message on this page, because it solves a problem they already have.

```
Subject: Freelance frontend capacity, Thrissur

Hi [name],

I am available for freelance and overflow work. If [agency name] ever has a frontend build that
needs an extra pair of hands, or a Next.js project nobody in-house wants to own, I would like to

Happy to do a small paid piece first so you can see how I work.

Nevil Krishna K
+91 92079 32070

## 4. Recruiter reply

**Channel**: LinkedIn message or email, replying to an approach.
**Opener**: thank them and answer in the same message. Recruiters move fast and a two-message
exchange loses the slot.
**When to use**: every recruiter approach, including for roles that are not a fit. A polite no
keeps the name in their system.

```
Hi [name], thanks for reaching out.

Quick answers so you have what you need:

Current role: Full Stack Developer at Lascade LLP, working on SeatInfo (Next.js 16 on Cloudflare
Workers, D1, R2, Durable Objects) and FlightPoints.
Experience: 3 years. Before Lascade, two years at Udyata Information Systems.
Stack: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Zustand, TanStack Query, Node.js, Kotlin with
Jetpack Compose, React Native, Cloudflare and AWS.
Location: Thrissur, Kerala. Open to remote, and to Kochi or Bengaluru for the right role.
Notice period: [fill in].
Expected: [fill in], open to discussing it against the scope.

Work and code: https://nevil.dev

Happy to talk this week. [Tuesday or Thursday after 6 PM] works, or send a slot and I will make
it work.
```

For a role that is not a fit:

```
Hi [name], thanks for thinking of me. This one is not a fit for me because [it is a pure
backend role / the location does not work].

I am a full stack developer in Thrissur, React and Next.js on the web and Kotlin on Android, 3
years in. If something closer comes up, please do come back to me: https://nevil.dev

Happy to refer someone if you tell me what you are looking for.
```

## 5. Follow-up after no answer

**Channel**: same channel as the original message. Never switch channels to chase someone.
**When to use**: day 3, then day 7. Then stop. Two follow-ups is persistence, four is a
nuisance.

**Day 3**

```
Hi [name], floating this back up in case it got buried. Should I send [the speed check /
the two talk options]?
```

**Day 7**

```
Hi [name], last one from me on this.

If it is not the right time, that is completely fine and I will not keep chasing. If it becomes
relevant later, I am at nevil.dev and on this number.

All the best with [their thing].
```

## 6. Quote follow-up

**Channel**: whichever one the quote was sent on.
**When to use**: 3 days after sending a quote with no reply. A quote that goes quiet usually
means one specific worry, and this message asks for it.

```
Hi [name], checking in on the quote for [the project].

If the number is the problem, tell me and I will show you what a smaller first version looks
like. Usually there is a version at [60%] of that which does the important part and can grow
later.

If it is the timeline or something else, same thing, just say it and I will work with it.

And if you have gone another way, no hard feelings at all, I would only ask you to tell me so I
stop following up.
```

## 7. Asking for a testimonial

**Channel**: WhatsApp for local clients, email for company contacts.
**When to use**: within a week of a delivery that went well, while it is fresh. Never months
later.

```
Hi [name], glad [the site / the panel] is working out.

Could you write me two or three lines I can put on my site? Just what you needed, what I built,
and whether it did the job. Your own words, and say anything critical if there is something.

If it is easier, answer these three and I will shape it into a sentence for you to approve:
1. What was the problem before?
2. What did I build?
3. What changed after?

And if you have two minutes for a Google review as well, that helps more than anything else:
[REVIEW LINK]

No pressure either way, thanks for the work.
```

Never write the testimonial and ask them to approve it without their input. A testimonial that
is really your own sentence with their name on it is a fabricated testimonial.

## 8. Asking for a referral

**Channel**: WhatsApp or a direct message, to a happy client or a friend who knows business
owners.
**When to use**: after a delivered project, or after someone has said something nice
unprompted. Ask once, then leave it.

```
Hi [name], a small favour.

I am taking on new projects right now. If you know anyone who needs [a website, an app or a
dashboard], would you pass my number along? [+91 92079 32070], and my work is at nevil.dev.

You do not need to sell me, just say I built [their thing] and that it worked.

If anything comes of it, dinner is on me.
```

long one with the speed-check offer is in `05-backlinks-and-lists.md`, priority 2.
**Subject**: `Addition for your "[exact post title]" post`
**When to use**: for any indexed listicle about developers in Thrissur, Kerala or India.

```

district. Useful list.

## 10. Offering a talk to a meetup organiser

**Channel**: whatever the community uses. Usually a Telegram or Discord message, or the
organiser's email.
**When to use**: whenever a community announces a call for speakers, and once unprompted per
community. The longer version is in `05`, priority 4.

```
Hi [name],

I am Nevil, a full stack developer in Thrissur. I maintain TMPlayer, an open source Telegram
video player for Android TV (Kotlin, Compose, TDLib, Media3).

I would like to speak at the next [community] meetup. Two options:

1. Running Next.js on Cloudflare Workers: what OpenNext does, what breaks, what it costs
   against Vercel. 20 minutes.
2. Rendering a 2,000 seat interactive map in the browser: SVG against canvas, hit testing, and
   getting it from 2.4s to 380ms. 20 minutes.

Slides and a live demo ready for either. Happy to do a 10 minute lightning version instead if
the slots are full.

No fee. I am in Thrissur, so anywhere between Palakkad and Ernakulam is easy.

https://nevil.dev
github.com/dracu-lah
```

## 11. Reply to a price-only enquiry

**Channel**: WhatsApp, usually. This is the most common first message a local business sends.
**When to use**: any version of "how much for a website". Answering with a number loses; the
number gets compared against a template site and the conversation ends. Answering with a
question keeps it alive.

```
Hi, thanks for messaging.

The honest answer is that it depends on what the site has to do, and I would rather give you a
real number than a made-up one. Three quick questions:

1. What is the site for, [selling online, getting enquiries, or just being findable]?
2. How many pages roughly, and do you already have the text and photos?
3. When do you need it live?

With those three answers I can give you a number and a timeline in one message.

For a rough sense: a simple business site is a few days of work, an online shop or a booking
system is a few weeks. I do not do 5,000 rupee template sites, and I will say so now rather
than after you have spent time on it.

If you are in Thrissur district, the first 20 minute call is free. Happy to do it on WhatsApp
right now if you have time.
```

If they push for a number before answering anything, give a range and a condition:

```
Understood. A range so you can decide whether to keep talking: a simple business site starts at
[amount] and a booking or ordering system starts at [amount]. Where it lands inside that depends
on the three questions above.
```
